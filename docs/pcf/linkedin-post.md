# LinkedIn Post: PCF Calculator & Validator Launch

We just shipped a Product Carbon Footprint (PCF) Calculator & Validator inside our open-source IDTA Submodel Template Editor.

Manufacturers are still stuck in spreadsheets for carbon data—hard to audit, impossible to trace. We built a workflow that turns activity data into a validated, machine‑readable PCF in seconds.

What it does:
- Add activities (electricity, transport, materials)
- Pick or enter emission factors with source, region, and year
- Auto-calc CO2e and write directly to the IDTA 02023 fields
- Live validation for mandatory fields + value list guidance
- Export with traceable calculation metadata and PCFActivities entries

The result: a PCF that is consistent, auditable, and ready for AAS-based digital twins.

Built with FastAPI + React/TypeScript on Eclipse BaSyx. If you're working on sustainability tooling, digital twins, or automated reporting, I'd love to compare notes.
